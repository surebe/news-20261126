One page. Every region. Every field. A single 1024×1024 front page that
tells the whole of **Thursday 26 November 2026** — built for the make.fun
bounty [news-image-20261126](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126).

## What's on the page

- **42 stories, 7 regions** — North America 6 · Latin America 3 ·
  Europe 8 · Middle East 5 · Africa 7 · Asia 9 · Oceania 4. Every story
  happened, or was first reported, on Thu 26 Nov 2026; anything older is
  named in the crimson **DATE CHECK** strip instead of being passed off
  as today's news.
- **The lead:** Houthi ballistic missiles fired at Riyadh for a third
  night — two destroyed by Saudi air defences, a tower in the financial
  district damaged, no injuries (coalition statement / civil defence).
- **The big numbers band:** 12,021 confirmed Ebola cases in DR Congo ·
  31 dead in a Cairo fuel-tanker fire · the €1.6tn EU budget fight ·
  1,000,000 satellites in SpaceX's cleared orbital data-centre plan ·
  76 hostages freed in Cartagena · 9 killed overnight in Gaza.
- **Certainty, labelled:** every lead carries a chip — CONFIRMED as
  reported, REPORTED single-source, CLAIM for one side's account — with
  the legend printed in the footer, and a source on every story.
- **The day on the map:** a dot-matrix world map with numbered pins for
  the located stories, plus an hour-by-hour UTC timeline of the day.

## How it was made

Stories were harvested from the wires (Reuters · AP · BBC · AFP ·
Al Jazeera · AAP · Kommersant) and date-checked one by one. The page is
typeset in HTML/CSS and rendered with WeasyPrint; the ten illustrations
are original flat-editorial artwork in a locked palette of ink black,
warm cream, crimson and slate. Headlines were shortened at selection
time so nothing on the page is ever clipped.
